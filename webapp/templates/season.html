Season tracker

Every finished game is scored against the forecast the model made before it was played: the win probability it gave, and how unusual the whole box score was. Then the game is folded into both teams' ratings, and the model is refit each night. At the start of the season the ratings are last season's, pulled part of the way back toward average; they sharpen as games are played. Upcoming games are forecast from the latest ratings.
