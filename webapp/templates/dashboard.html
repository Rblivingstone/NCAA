Bayesian updates

After each final, the box score updates both teams' 22 latent skills: the prior (the belief before the game) times the likelihood of that box score gives the posterior. Net rating = expected points per 100 possessions against an average D1 team.

Win/loss prediction: every game

Every game as a yes/no prediction of "the first-listed team wins" (the home team, or the first one named at a neutral site), called for whichever side the model gave more than 50%. Precision: when the model called a result, how often it happened. Recall: of the games that ended that way, how many the model called. ROC-AUC: the chance a random winner got a higher pre-game score than a random loser (0.5 = no skill, 1 = perfect).

Upset detection

Treating "will the underdog win?" as a yes/no prediction. A game counts when the ranking before it made one team the underdog (20+ places apart, or a non-D1 team); the model's score is its pre-game chance for that underdog. Precision: of the games it flagged, how many were upsets. Recall: of the upsets, how many it flagged. ROC-AUC: the chance a random upset got a higher pre-game score than a random non-upset (0.5 = no skill, 1 = perfect).
