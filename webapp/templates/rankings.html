Team rankings

Every team's posterior skills turned into expected points per 100 possessions against an average Division I opponent on a neutral floor. Overall is the net rating (offense minus defense), Offense ranks points scored per 100 possessions, Defense ranks points allowed (lower is better). Intervals are 90% credible intervals. During a season every team ranked is a current Division I team, and its rating is updated (Bayesian) as soon as each of its games goes final; Moved is how far those updates have shifted it this season.
