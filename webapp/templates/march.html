Road to March

The rest of the {{ season_label }} season, simulated thousands of times from the current ratings. Each simulation plays every remaining game, every conference tournament, picks the NCAA field and plays the tournament, with the uncertainty in every team's rating carried through. The odds below are the share of simulations in which each thing happened. They are refreshed every night.

Chance to win the national title, with the chance to reach the Final Four and to make the field behind it. Hover a bar for the numbers.

The 68 teams most likely to be selected, in order of their average projected seed. Real seeding also follows rules this doesn't model (conference separation, geography), so treat lines as approximate.
How it works, and what it simplifies
  • Every game is a full simulated game from the model, the same one the rest of the site uses, with home court where it applies. Each simulation uses one sample of every team's skills, so a team the model is unsure about gets appropriately wide odds.
  • Conference tournaments: every member, seeded by simulated conference record, single elimination on a neutral floor, top seeds getting byes (the Ivy League takes its top four). Real formats vary a little.
  • Selection: conference tournament champions get the automatic bids. The remaining at-large places go to the best other teams by a résumé score that weighs strength and winning percentage equally. That's a stand-in for the selection committee, which can't be modelled exactly.
  • Seeding: down an S-curve by the same score, with the First Four for the four lowest automatic bids and the last four at-large teams. Conference-separation and geographic rules are not modelled.
