Team ratings

Posterior means of each team's latent skills entering the tournament, translated into expected points per 100 possessions against an average Division I opponent on a neutral floor. Net rating includes a 90% credible interval. Season trend is the change in net rating since opening night (green/red when the 90% interval excludes zero). Rates are the team's offensive rates against an average defense.
