Team ratings

Posterior means of each team's latent skills entering the tournament, translated into expected points per 100 possessions against an average Division I opponent on a neutral floor. Net rating includes a 90% credible interval. Season trend is how much the team's form (points per 100 possessions) changed since the opening weeks, green/red when its 90% interval excludes zero; the sparkline traces form across the season in two-week steps, ending at the tournament (form there is zero by definition, so the line shows how much better or worse the team used to be). Rates are the team's offensive rates against an average defense.
